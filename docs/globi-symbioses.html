GloBI symbioses

What to look under or on to find a useful catalogue species: symbiotic partners recorded in Global Biotic Interactions, kept when they help find one (for a fungus, the plants it grows under or on; for a plant or seaweed, the useful catalogue species that grow with it). Of the 478 catalogue species, only useful ones are listed (not do-not-eat, or with a non-food use), and only partners named to species that GBIF has at least 1 occurrence(s) of in New Zealand: 860 partners across 98 species. Bold partners are catalogue species. Each partner links to its records on GloBI; hover for counts. GloBI is worldwide and aggregated, and a GBIF occurrence can be a garden plant or a border interception, so a pair is a lead to check, not a New Zealand record of the partnership.

No GloBI records at all for 14 species (it may know them under another name): arthropodium-bifurcatum, asplenium-lepidotum, beach-spinach, bush-nettle, caulerpa-parvifolia, durvillaea-chathamensis, durvillaea-willana, gingidia-grisea, kanono, karengo, mountain-flax, mowers-mushroom, oxalis-rubens, pekepeke-kiore.
